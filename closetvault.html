LOW ENERGY · INDEPENDENTLY TESTED
Closet reality check

Any powered dehumidifier or heater rejects heat. A tight, unventilated closet can trap it. The cabinet needs manufacturer/engineering-approved airflow paths, anti-tip support, moisture containment and service access.
